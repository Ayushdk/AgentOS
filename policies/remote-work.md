## [schedule] Hybrid schedule
<!-- claim: remote_days=2 -->
Eligible employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager agreement. Teams choose office days together.

## [security] Secure remote access
Use a company-managed device and approved VPN for remote access. Never copy customer financial data to personal storage.

## [exceptions] Remote work exceptions
Working from another country or extending remote work beyond two days requires a People Operations review. No automatic exception is available.
