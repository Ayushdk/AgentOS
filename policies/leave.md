# Leave & time away

Fictional Meridian Ledger policy · synthetic portfolio data.

## [entitlement] Annual leave entitlement
<!-- claim: annual_days=18 -->
Annual leave provides 18 working days per calendar year. Sick leave provides 8 working days. Balances are maintained by People Operations. Pending requests reserve available days; approval deducts those days from the remaining balance.

## [working-week] Working days and holidays
The working week is Monday through Friday in Asia/Kolkata. Weekends and company holidays do not use leave. Meridian Foundation Day is November 18, 2026. A November 16–20, 2026 request uses four working days.

## [approval] Confirmation and manager approval
Employees must confirm the exact dated draft before submitting. Submission creates a pending request. A direct manager separately approves or rejects it. A draft expires after 30 minutes. Calendar conflicts are shown; meetings are never changed automatically.

## [eligibility] Validation and availability
Only annual and sick leave are supported. Leave cannot overlap pending or approved leave, start in the past, exceed available balance, or span more than 91 calendar days. Requests with no working days are not accepted. Exceptions need People Operations review.
